## Problem Statement
Hiring platforms hide names at the resume stage, but identity returns once interviews start, which is where the bias benefit fades. Hireva keeps the candidate anonymous through screening, interview and tests, and reveals identity only after selection. Goal: a hobby project with real social meaning, not a company yet. No demand evidence exists yet ("No one yet").

## What Makes This Cool
Anonymity that survives the live rounds. The agent interviewer is anonymous by construction, and the recruiter only ever sees a scrubbed transcript.

## Constraints
- Hobby project, solo, no customers, 2 to 3 weekends for v1.
- Gender and accent leaks accepted for now. The promise is "name, school, employer and identity details are hidden", not "nothing can be inferred".
- No video, no screen sharing. Code goes in a shared text box.
- Writing-style and code-style leaks accepted for now.

## Premises (all agreed)
1. Resume anonymization is a crowded commodity. The differentiator is anonymity through interview and test rounds.
2. v1 is not the full pipeline. It is a candidate-side leak linter plus an anonymous voice interview with an AI agent and a scrubbed transcript scored by a recruiter.
3. Voice alteration is out of v1 (gender/accent leaks accepted).
4. Identity is revealed only after selection and is never used during evaluation.
5. Success is a working demo you can show, possibly open-sourced, not revenue.

## Landscape
Resume-stage blind screening: Pinpoint, Applied, GapJumpers, Blendoor, TalVista, MeVitae. Interview-stage: Sapia (text interviews), Interview.io (voice masking). Open source: resume-anonymizer (npm, license unverified). Known pitfalls: over-redaction hurts judging, indirect clues (regional college, volunteer group) still leak, benefit fades at interview stage.

## Approaches Considered
- A: Leak linter only. Rejected as v1: ships fast but sits in the crowded resume space and skips the differentiator. Kept as a component of B.
- B: Anonymous agent interview with scrubbed transcript (chosen).
- C: Bias mirror (reveal and compare). Deferred: needs real usage data and careful ethics.

## Recommended Approach
B. Candidate runs the leak linter on their portfolio, then talks by voice (plus the shared code box; no text chat) with an agent interviewer persona. Every spoken answer is transcribed and the transcript scrubbed for identity clues before storage; the audio is discarded. A recruiter opens a link and scores the scrubbed transcript against a fixed rubric. Identity stays hidden until selection.

## Open Questions
- Tech stack is undecided.
- How to scrub live chat reliably (NER plus LLM pass, or rules plus review) and how to show candidates what was removed.
- Rubric design for the agent interviewer and the recruiter scoring.
- Is resume-anonymizer reusable, and what is its license?
- Whether the human interviewer round needs real-time scrubbing or stays out of v1.

## Success Criteria
Two people, one link: a candidate completes an interview with an agent, and a second person scores the scrubbed transcript without being able to identify the candidate's name, school or employer. At least one outsider tries it.

## Distribution Plan
Web app, deploy to a free host. Open-source repo if the scrubber holds up. Not decided.
